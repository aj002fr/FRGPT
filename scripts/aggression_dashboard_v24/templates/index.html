How to read this

ARMED: the signal fired on the last completed bar. Paper entry happens at the next bar, never the signal bar — entering at the signal bar's own price is look-ahead and inflates results.

Gross vs net ticks: net subtracts the round-trip cost shown in the spec. Only net matters. A 0.5-tick assumption is already optimistic for TY, where the resting spread is typically a full tick.

Two books: the offline study found the LIFO gate helped in-sample and hurt out-of-sample. Both books run on the same stream so forward evidence can settle which is right.

Exit mix: how trades ended — target, stop, or the time stop at the horizon.
