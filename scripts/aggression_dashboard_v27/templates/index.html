What these stats mean

ENTRY: fade when |z| ≥ 1.5. EXIT Z-RESET: the fade side dies (short exits at z ≤ 0.5, long at z ≥ −0.5) after 5 minutes. EXIT FLIP: the other side hits |z| ≥ 1, even during the min-hold. 10M MARK: score/display only — not a flatten. The trade stays open until z-reset or flip.

Session day: ticks captured and counts from the last 18:00 ET Globex open through now. Today’s table uses that same window, not the calendar date. Open trades are counted but not included in ticks until they close.

Trade pnl: fade P&L from that trade’s entry print to its exit print. Each entry→exit is one row. A new trade can open after the previous one closes.

Trade adverse: worst fade-path mark between that trade’s entry and its own exit.

Hold Expectations: median time for resolved alerts to close, median adverse ticks before resolution, and the share of reversion caused by price moving rather than fair value drifting.
