What these stats mean

Resolved: the signal's absolute z-score dropped back below 1.0 before the 30-minute deadline. Expired: it did not.

30m hit / 30m pnl: whether fading the alert direction made money after exactly 30 minutes, and by how many price points. This is the trade scoreboard.

30m adverse: the worst price move against the fade during the first 30 minutes, even if the alert later worked.

Hold Expectations: median time for resolved alerts to close, median adverse ticks before resolution, and the share of reversion caused by price moving rather than fair value drifting.
