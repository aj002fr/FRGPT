The numbers in the middle are history: how much more buy, sell, and net an auction day has had than a normal day at that same clock. Auction-day move is the median price change on those days. Implied by auction net is the normal-day line, intercept plus slope times that net. This week, when it is a 2/5/7 week, sits above those. A clock that has not opened says when it opens. An open clock is so-far flow and updates with the 1m tape. It joins the historical median only after the clock finishes. Buy took the ask. Sell hit the bid.
