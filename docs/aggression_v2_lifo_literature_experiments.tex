\subsection{How a result is measured}

Let \(B\) be the nonevent, complete-horizon baseline zone rate on that
split (2026-09-07 validation \(B \approx 8.5\%\) after 15-minute
decluster; recompute, do not hard-code).

For each flag \(F\):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Restrict to nonevent, complete 15-minute rows.
    \item Decluster at 15 minutes (\code{EPISODE_STRIDE_MIN}, same
    helper as 2026-09-07).
    \item \(\widehat{p} = P(\code{lifo\_zone\_15m}=1 \mid F=1)\).
    \item Lift \(= 100\times(\widehat{p}-B)\) in percentage points.
    \item Date-clustered bootstrap, \(N=400\), one-sided \(p\) that
    lift $>0$ (or $<0$ for a veto).  Same
    \code{aggression_gate_lib} helpers as the zone study.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Letter-pass (validation, primary flag only):}
\(n \ge 50\), dates \(\ge 8\), lift \(\ge +8\)\,pp, one-sided 95\% CI
above 0, and discovery lift has the same sign.  FINAL is read only
after that letter.  A veto letter-passes on lift \(\le -8\)\,pp with
CI below 0.

Also print the three pieces (meat / contained / two-way) so a lift
cannot hide as ``more range, no reversal.''  The 2026-09-07 binding
constraint was two-way (\(\approx 10.3\%\)), not meat (\(51\%\)).

\textbf{Not a pass:} hit rate on an edge clock with \(n<50\); in-sample
\(R^2\); live dashboard rates.

\section{What H5 already taught (do not repeat)}

H5 = \(\abs{z_{\mathrm{V1}}}\ge 1\) and
\code{trade\_intensity} \(\le\) session expanding \(p_{33}\).
Validation zone rate \(4.2\%\) vs \(8.5\%\) ($-4.3$\,pp).  Quiet
intensity did the damage.  BuySell-ratio flips on that quiet tape
made it worse.  High intensity was slightly \emph{more} zonal.
Branch C on H5 had validation \(n=3\).

So the next flags must live in \textbf{moderate or busy} minutes, and
an edge clock is only scored if it actually fires.

\section{L1 --- moderate two-sided flow
(Kyle / VPIN / Andersen / Avellaneda arrivals)}

\paragraph{Steal.}
A clip is a noise-trader / two-sided-volume state, not a quiet leftover.
Andersen: the two-sided piece must add something after you already
know intensity.

\paragraph{Causal features, trailing, no future bars.}

Session expanding quantiles use completed minutes on that ET date
only, warmup 20 minutes (same as 2026-09-07).

\begin{align}
I_t &= \code{trade\_intensity}_t, \\
q^{I}_{33,t},\; q^{I}_{67,t}
 &= \text{expanding } p_{33},\; p_{67}\text{ of }I
 \text{ on the ET date}, \\
\text{\code{int\_mid}}_t
 &= \mathbf{1}\{q^{I}_{33,t} < I_t \le q^{I}_{67,t}\}.
\end{align}

Two-sidedness over a trailing window \(W=15\) (primary):
\begin{align}
A_{t,j} &= \code{agg\_1m}_{t-j}, \qquad
S_t = \sum_{j=0}^{14} A_{t,j}, \qquad
D_t = \sum_{j=0}^{14} \abs{A_{t,j}}, \\
\nu_t &= \abs{S_t}/D_t \quad (D_t>0;\ \text{else NaN}), \\
\text{\code{twosided}}_t
 &= \mathbf{1}\{\nu_t \le q^{\nu}_{33,t}\}.
\end{align}
\(\nu=0\) is perfectly matched signed flow; \(\nu=1\) is one-sided.
This is VPIN's imbalance idea on aggression, in clock time, because
the published panel has no buy/sell contract split.

\paragraph{Primary flag (starred).}
\[
\text{\code{mod\_twosided}}_t
 = \text{\code{int\_mid}}_t \land \text{\code{twosided}}_t.
\]

\paragraph{Discovery-only sensitivities (do not pick a winner on validation).}
\(W\in\{5,15\}\); two-sided cut \(p_{25}\) vs \(p_{33}\);
\code{int\_mid} vs ``not quiet'' (\(I_t > q^{I}_{33,t}\)).
Volume-clock variant: fill buckets with cumulative
\code{vol\_per\_min} until \(V^{\ast}=2000\) contracts, then
\(\nu^{\mathrm{vol}}=\abs{\sum \code{agg\_1m}}/\sum\abs{\code{agg\_1m}}\)
inside the completed bucket; two-sided = session expanding \(p_{33}\)
of that \(\nu^{\mathrm{vol}}\).  \(V^{\ast}\) is frozen; do not search it
on validation.

\paragraph{Andersen control (required print, not a second gate).}
Inside \code{int\_mid} only, lift of \code{twosided} vs not-twosided.
If \code{mod\_twosided} beats the baseline only because it dropped the
quiet tercile, L1 fails the control even if the headline lift is
positive.

\paragraph{Output table (validation, then discovery; FINAL locked).}
Flag \(n\), dates, zone rate, lift pp, CI, meat / two-way / path
rates, and the Andersen within-tercile lift.

\paragraph{Pass / fail.}
Letter-pass as in the shared gate.  Predicted direction: positive lift
on \code{mod\_twosided}; H5 remains negative.  A pass does not ship a
banner.

\section{L2 --- absorption at a swing extreme
(Cont / Potter)}

\paragraph{Steal.}
Large flow, small \(\Delta P\), at a level that already moved 3 ticks.
That is an edge at a \emph{level}, not a day-long zone banner.
H5 never sat on a 3-tick push (\(n=3\)).  This experiment is allowed
to fail for \(n\).

\paragraph{Absorption bar (same minute, Cont-style).}
\begin{align}
\Delta P^{\mathrm{ticks}}_t
 &= (\code{price}_t - \code{price}_{t-1}) / (1/64), \\
\text{\code{absorb\_bar}}_t
 &= \mathbf{1}\!\left\{
 \abs{\code{agg\_1m}_t} \ge q^{\abs{A}}_{67,t}
 \;\land\;
 \abs{\Delta P^{\mathrm{ticks}}_t} \le 1
 \right\}.
\end{align}
This is a 1-minute signed-flow proxy for OFI-without-\(\Delta P\).
It is not true L1 OFI.

\paragraph{Extreme (already on the 2026-09-07 panel).}
\code{trail\_meat\_15m}: completed trailing 15-minute high$-$low
\(\ge 3\) ticks.  Last-push sign:
\(\code{fade\_last\_push\_sign}_t = -\mathrm{sign}(\code{mom\_5m}_t)\)
when \(\abs{\code{mom\_5m}_t}/(1/64)\ge 3\), else 0.

\paragraph{Primary flag.}
\[
\text{\code{absorb\_extreme}}_t
 = \text{\code{absorb\_bar}}_t
 \land \text{\code{trail\_meat\_15m}}_t
 \land (\code{fade\_last\_push\_sign}_t \ne 0).
\]
Diagnostic (not gated): also require
\code{displayed\_depth\_mean} \(\ge\) session expanding \(p_{50}\).

\paragraph{Two outputs.}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Environment:} same zone-rate lift as L1 on
    \code{absorb\_extreme}.
    \item \textbf{Edge clock:} when the flag is on, fade the last
    3-tick / 5-minute push for 10 minutes.  Hit $=1$ if $+3$ ticks in
    the fade direction prints before $-2$ against
    (\code{fade_path_hit} from the 2026-09-07 script).  Decluster
    15 minutes.  Report \(n\), hit rate, and date-clustered CI.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Pass / fail.}
Environment letter uses the shared $+8$\,pp gate.  Edge letter
requires validation \(n\ge 50\) \emph{and} hit rate \(\ge 0.60\) with
one-sided CI above \(0.50\).  If \(n<50\), write \texttt{FAIL\_N} and
do not discuss the hit rate.  Do not fall back onto H5 to manufacture
episodes.

\section{L3 --- sign-run environment
(Bouchaud / Tsaknaki)}

\paragraph{Steal.}
Persistent same-sign aggression is the sliced-meta-order state
(stand aside).  A 3-tick box lives in flippy-but-not-dead flow.
Do \emph{not} implement BOCPD / MBOC in this pass; the stealable
object is the run length.

\paragraph{Causal run length.}
Let \(s_t = \mathrm{sign}(\code{agg\_1m}_t)\), with \(s_t=0\) when
\(\code{agg\_1m}_t=0\).  On each ET date:
\begin{align}
R_t &=
\begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } s_t=0, \\
1 & \text{if } s_t \ne 0 \text{ and } s_t \ne s_{t-1}, \\
R_{t-1}+1 & \text{if } s_t = s_{t-1} \ne 0.
\end{cases}
\end{align}

\paragraph{Primary flags.}
\begin{align}
\text{\code{metaorder\_run}}_t
 &= \mathbf{1}\{R_t \ge 10\}, \\
\text{\code{flippy\_active}}_t
 &= \mathbf{1}\{1 \le R_t \le 2\}
 \land \mathbf{1}\{I_t > q^{I}_{33,t}\}.
\end{align}

\paragraph{Output.}
Zone-rate lift for both flags.  Predicted signs:
\code{flippy\_active} positive, \code{metaorder\_run} negative (veto).
Also print mean \(\abs{\text{net}}_{15\mathrm{m}}\) and two-way rate
inside each flag (Tsaknaki diagnostic: long runs should look like
one-way concave flow, not a box).

\paragraph{Pass / fail.}
\code{flippy\_active} uses the shared $+8$\,pp letter.
\code{metaorder\_run} uses the veto letter ($-8$\,pp).
Either can pass alone.  Discovery-only sensitivity: run cutoff
\(\{8,10,15\}\).

\section{L4 --- Hasbrouck measurement (no flag, no gate)}

\paragraph{Steal.}
A trade shock has a permanent piece and a dying piece.  Report whether
large \(\code{agg\_1m}\) minutes \emph{retrace} over the next 15 minutes.
This is a measurement, not a banner candidate.

\paragraph{Procedure.}
On nonevent complete rows, tercile \(\abs{\code{agg\_1m}}\) with the
session expanding \(p_{33}/p_{67}\).  In each tercile report:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \(P(\code{lifo\_zone\_15m})\), \(P(\text{two-way})\),
    \(P(\text{meat})\);
    \item median \(\abs{\text{endpoint}}_{15\mathrm{m}}\) in ticks;
    \item retrace share:
    \(\abs{\text{endpoint}} / \max_k\abs{\text{path}_k}\)
    (1 $=$ the 15-minute move stayed; 0 $=$ it came all the way back).
\end{itemize}

Optional VAR-lite (discovery only): residualize \(\code{agg\_1m}\) on
five lags of itself and of \(\Delta P\), then repeat on the residual
terciles.  Do not turn those residuals into a live \(z\).

\paragraph{How to read it.}
If the high-\(\abs{\code{agg}}\) tercile has \emph{lower} two-way and
higher retrace share, aggression is more permanent --- L2 fades are
the wrong play on those bars, and L3's long run is the veto.
If two-way is flat or higher in the busy tercile, that supports L1
(swings need prints).  No letter-pass.  Print the table and stop.

\subsection{Flow forecast vs two-stage price map}

Script: \code{scripts/aggression_v2_lifo_flow_forecast_research.py}.
Outputs: \code{workspace/analysis/aggression_v2_lifo_flow_forecast/}.
Past windows \(W\in\{1,5,15,30\}\), forward \(H\in\{1,5,15\}\).
\code{ship\_gate} false.

Past signed flow does not forecast future signed flow.  Validation
flow\(\to\)flow \(R^2\) is at most \(0.003\) (\(W=5\), \(H=5\));
sign hit is a coin flip at every cell.  Stacking all four lookbacks
does not help (validation \(H=1\) forecast \(R^2=-0.003\)).

If future flow were known, mapping it to price is easy and gets
easier as you integrate: oracle \(R^2(\Delta P,\text{future flow})\)
is \(0.25\) / \(0.40\) / \(0.52\) at \(H=1/5/15\) on validation.
That is Cont's contemporaneous object over a longer window, not a
forecast.  Direct past-flow\(\to\)future-\(\Delta P\) and two-stage
\(\lambda\times\hat F\) are both \(\approx 0\) out of sample.  The
bottleneck is stage 1.  Predicted flow does not make the price map
easier because there is no predicted flow.
