\begin{abstract}
This note describes only Aggression Monitor V2.7
(\code{scripts/aggression_dashboard_v27/}, port 5063,
\url{https://aasthajain.tail174727.ts.net:8445/}).
The useful job is V1's measurement question on a short window: did
the last ten minutes of TY match the last ten minutes of aggressive
curve flow and quote-at-trade context?  The dashed line is a
contemporaneous nowcast of that stretch, not a price target and not
a forecast of the next ten minutes.  A paper fade clock is also on
the page; that clock is a diagnostic scoreboard hung on the leftover,
not the reason the monitor exists.  The page does not emit executable
long or short, does not pass an after-cost gate, and does not write
to any other aggression monitor.

Fair value is the frozen short-horizon construction \code{B1_W10}:
the last print ten minutes earlier, plus a Ridge fit of that
ten-minute price change on a short flow pack.  The residual of that
fit is standardized with a historical prior blend.  A paper trade may
open only when local \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) \emph{and} live V2's session
residual is 2--6 ticks with the same sign and a new session-sign after
the last exit.  The fade-side exit is \(\abs{z}\) through 0.5 after a
five-minute min-hold, or a flip at opposite \(\abs{z}\ge 1\).  Ten
minutes is a score and display mark, not a flatten.

The fixed status line is
\code{PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH-ONLY / LOCAL-10M-FV / NOT AFTER-COST VALIDATED}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Goal}

V1 asks whether \emph{this session's} TY move is in the aggressive
curve prints.  V2.7 asks the same question on a shorter stretch:
did the last ten minutes of TY match the last ten minutes of those
prints?

That is a consistency check, not a valuation.  The dashed line is
``where the last print would sit if the last ten minutes of price
were a linear function of the short flow-and-book pack.''  When
solid price and the dash travel together, the stretch is explained
by prints we already have.  When they diverge, the last ten minutes
contain leftover price change the pack does not account for.  New
aggressive flow is allowed to rewrite the line on the next minute:
\code{agg_1m} updates, the nowcast moves, and the leftover can die
because the model chased the print.  That is the construction
working.  It is not a failed fade.

The page is helpful in four situations:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{A short burst that V1's session line is too slow
    to comment on.}  TY has moved a few ticks in the last ten
    minutes.  V1's session residual and 15-row banner are still
    about the day.  V2.7 says whether \emph{this} burst was paid
    for in recent curve flow.
    \item \textbf{An unexplained local stretch.}  Price ran while
    recent \code{agg_1m}/\code{agg_5m} was quiet, opposite, or in
    the wings.  The leftover grows.  The working idea: something
    other than the last ten minutes of prints is carrying TY.
    \item \textbf{A flow-explained local stretch.}  The dash stays
    close to last print while flow is one-sided.  The working idea:
    do not invent a fade; the move is in the tape.
    \item \textbf{An impulse that just arrived.}  Fresh size hits,
    the nowcast jumps toward price, and local \(z\) collapses.
    The working idea: the last ten minutes are being rewritten.
    Watch the new line; do not treat the old leftover as a target.
\end{itemize}

The page is not helpful as a place price should go back to, as a
forecast of the next ten minutes, or as a long/short.  Track~A's
forward \(R^2\) lift versus random walk was of order \(10^{-5}\).
Fade-the-local-residual hit rates stayed near 51--52 percent.
Usable 5--15~minute clocks had median percent-via-price \(=0\):
the gap closed because the line moved.

What was implemented is a copy of V2.2 with session Ridge replaced
by this 10-minute nowcast, plus a paper ENTRY / EXIT clock and the
LIFO FRIENDLY overlay.  That clock is a diagnostic scoreboard hung
on the leftover (does the gap later shrink, and did price or the
line do the work?).  It is not the goal.  Named V2.7 because V2.3
already belongs to the move-exhaustion monitor on port 5059.

The 2026-08-31 study found a stability cell for watching the
leftover without two-minute chatter, not a tradable edge.  The
2026-09-02 tape then showed most local \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) bars were
nowcast-only, so the live clock also requires a 2--6 tick session
residual with the same sign.  That filter cuts chatter; it does not
turn the leftover into a target.  \code{ship_gate} remains false.

\section{Fair value}

\subsection{Why local, not session}

Live V2 / V2.2 fair value is session-from-open:

\begin{equation}
  y^{\mathrm{session}}_t = P_t - P_{\mathrm{open}},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{FV}^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  = P_{\mathrm{open}} + \mathrm{Ridge}(X^{\mathrm{session}}_t,\, y^{\mathrm{session}}).
\end{equation}

That residual is a session-scale level.  Raw sign of that residual
flipped about every two minutes on the 2026-08-31 holdout (7.0
flips/hour).  Track~A, a forward Ridge of \(P_{t+h}-P_t\), had
sign skill (1-minute AUC 0.67) but \(R^2\) lift versus random walk of
order \(10^{-5}\).  It does not draw a useful new price line.

Track~B asked the question this page is actually for: can a rolling
anchor plus a short pack \emph{explain the last} \(W\) minutes of
price?  That is V1's measurement job on a local window.  The frozen
cell is \(W=10\) with pack B1.  It was not chosen because ten
minutes is a true fair-value horizon.  It was the window where the
nowcast still fits the stretch just taken without the \(W=5\)
\code{mom_5m} leak, and where a hysteresis lean lasts long enough
to look at.

\subsection{B1\_W10}

Let \(P_t\) be the TY last print in the completed 1-minute bar.  The
local anchor is the print ten minutes earlier:

\begin{equation}
  A_t = P_{t-10},
  \qquad
  y^{\mathrm{B1}}_t = P_t - A_t.
\end{equation}

The first ten bars of a session have no anchor and produce no
prediction.  There is no lookahead: \(A_t\) is a shift of \(P\).

Fair value and residual:

\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\Delta}_t = \mathrm{RidgeCV}(X^{\mathrm{B1}}_t,\, y^{\mathrm{B1}}),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{FV}_t = A_t + \widehat{\Delta}_t,
  \qquad
  e_t = P_t - \mathrm{FV}_t.
\end{equation}

Positive \(e_t\): last print is rich versus the 10-minute nowcast.
Negative \(e_t\): cheap.  The paper lean fades \(e_t\): short when
rich, long when cheap.

RidgeCV uses the live V2 alphas \(\{0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100\}\) behind
a StandardScaler.  Training keeps the V2 cleanliness filter: at least
500 rows per ET date, price in \((80, 150)\), session target
standard deviation in \((0.01, 1)\).  Fit is once per UTC calendar
date on that historical matrix, not on the live buffer.

\code{agg_30m} and \code{minutes_since_open} are excluded.
\code{W=5} was rejected in research because live \code{mom_5m}
equals the local target at that window (specification leak,
\(R^2=1\)).  \(W=10\) does not have that leak.

\subsection{Residual \(z\)-score}

Alerts use \(z\), not raw \(e\).  Method \code{prior_blend_v1}:

\begin{align}
  \mu_{\mathrm{hist}} &= \text{training-residual mean}, \\
  \sigma_{\mathrm{hist}} &= \text{median of per-day residual std.\ (positive days)}, \\
  w_t &= \frac{60}{60 + n_t},
\end{align}

where \(n_t\) is the expanding count of live residuals so far today.
The live mean and std.\ use an expanding window with a 30-point
minimum.  Until that minimum, the historical moments stand in.

\begin{equation}
  \mu_t = w_t\,\mu_{\mathrm{hist}} + (1-w_t)\,\mu^{\mathrm{today}}_t,
  \qquad
  \sigma_t^2 = w_t\,\sigma_{\mathrm{hist}}^2 + (1-w_t)\,(\sigma^{\mathrm{today}}_t)^2,
  \qquad
  z_t = \frac{e_t - \mu_t}{\sigma_t}.
\end{equation}

Local residuals are typically sub-tick to about one tick.  A large
\(z\) can therefore be a small economic gap.  Treat \(z\) as a
stability score, not a dollar dislocation.

Live banner states from \(\abs{z}\) alone (before the conviction
gate):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\(\abs{z}\) & State \\
\midrule
\(< 1.5\) & \code{NONE} \\
\(\ge 1.5\) & \code{WATCH} \\
\(\ge 2.0\) & \code{DISLOCATION} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\code{WATCH} and \code{DISLOCATION} are labels on the same clock.
A WATCH can escalate to DISLOCATION if \(\abs{z}\) later reaches 2.
They are not two strategies.


\section{Paper alert clock}

One episode may be open at a time.  TY tick size is \(1/64\).
Direction is fade:

\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{sign}
  =
  \begin{cases}
    -1 & e_t > 0 \quad (\mathrm{SHORT\_RICH}), \\
    +1 & e_t < 0 \quad (\mathrm{LONG\_CHEAP}).
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

Paper P\&L on a path is \(\mathrm{sign}\times(P-P_{\mathrm{open}})\).
Positive means the fade made last-print ticks.

\subsection{Conviction entry}

Local \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) is necessary and not sufficient.  Let
\(e^{\mathrm{sess}}_t\) be V2's session residual on the same minute,
and \(\tau_t = e^{\mathrm{sess}}_t / (1/64)\) its tick value.  A new
episode opens only when all of the following hold and no episode is
already open:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Session residual is present (else \code{SESSION_UNAVAILABLE}).
    \item \(2 \le \abs{\tau_t} \le 6\)
    (else \code{NOWCAST_ONLY} if too small, \code{REGIME_TAIL} if too large).
    \item \(\mathrm{sign}(e_t)=\mathrm{sign}(e^{\mathrm{sess}}_t)\)
    and neither is zero (else \code{DISAGREE}).
    \item That session sign is not the same as the last finalized
    episode's exit session-sign in the current 18:00~ET Globex session
    (else \code{SAME_SIGN_OPEN}).
\end{enumerate}

The live strip then shows
\code{FLAT --- SKIP <reason>}
when \(z\) is hot but gated, or the wait text
``FLAT --- wait for \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\), session 2--6 ticks, same sign,
new session-sign after exit.''

This gate is an alert-rate filter from the 2026-09-02 correction tape
/ L1 autopsy (52 live trades; 31 nowcast-only; most \(z\)-resets had
percent-via-price \(\le 0\)).  It is not an after-cost PASS.  C1
(\(\abs{\tau}\ge 2\)) was persistence on that tape, not expectancy.

\subsection{Exit}

Once open:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{EXIT Z-RESET.}
    After five minutes, the fade side dies:
    short exits at \(z\le 0.5\), long at \(z\ge -0.5\).
    \item \textbf{EXIT FLIP.}
    Opposite \(\abs{z}\ge 1\), even during the five-minute min-hold.
    Short flips at \(z\le -1\); long flips at \(z\ge +1\).
    \item \textbf{10M MARK.}
    Ten minutes after entry is scored (5-minute and 10-minute path
    marks) and drawn on the chart.  The trade stays open.  A
    \(z\)-reset or flip still closes it after the mark.
\end{itemize}

A closed trade frees the next entry, subject to the same-sign lockout
for the rest of the 18:00 session.  The lockout is on session-residual
sign, not on local \(z\).

\subsection{Paper scores}

Each entry\(\rightarrow\)exit is one row.  Table \textbf{trade pnl} is
last-print fade from the open last print to the exit last print.
\textbf{Trade adverse} is the worst fade-path mark between those two
prints.

The session-day banner sums \code{dir_pnl_trade_ticks} over finalized
rows in the visible window.  Open trades count as issued/open and do
not enter ticks until they close.  Win / lose / flat split those
closed tick values at zero.

Hold-expectation cards report, for resolved episodes: median minutes
to close, median adverse ticks before resolution, and price share of
reversion

\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{share}
  =
  \mathrm{clip}_{[-1,2]}
  \left(
    \mathrm{sign}(e_{\mathrm{open}})
    \frac{P_{\mathrm{open}}-P_{\mathrm{close}}}{\abs{e_{\mathrm{open}}}}
  \right)
\end{equation}

when \(\abs{e_{\mathrm{open}}}\) is at least one tick.  Share near 0
means the nowcast moved to price (FV chase).  Share near 1 means price
moved to the nowcast (the fade the clock claims).

\subsection{Session-day window}

Globex session open is the most recent 18:00~ET at or before now.
Same-sign re-entry always uses that full session.

The banner and Today's table use a one-session cutover: for the
Globex day that contains 2026-09-02 12:07~ET, visible stats start
there (conviction-gate restart).  After the next 18:00~ET open they
use the full trading day again.


\section{LIFO FRIENDLY}

Display-only, inherited from V2.2 / foundations H5.  Not a second
entry clock.  Not long or short.

Rule, on V2.7's own local \(z\) and V2 trade intensity:

\begin{equation}
  \abs{z_t}\ge 1
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \text{trade intensity}_t
  \le
  \text{session expanding } p_{33}
  \text{ (prior bars, min.\ 20)}.
\end{equation}

UI text is only \textbf{LIFO FRIENDLY}.  Label
\code{PROVISIONAL / LIFO-FRIENDLY / RANGE-LEAN / NON-DIRECTIONAL}.
Validation had a restrictive PASS; FINAL one-sided CI failed.  Live
counts are observational.  They do not promote the overlay.

\section{How to read the page}

These are reading habits, not validated states.  Read the leftover
as ``explained vs unexplained last ten minutes,'' not as a fade.

\subsection{Flow-explained stretch}

Last print and the dashed nowcast travel together.  Local \(z\) is
inside 1.5.  Recent curve flow has the same sign as the ten-minute
price change.  The working idea: this burst is in the prints.  The
useful action is to stop looking for a fade, not to enter one.

\subsection{Unexplained local stretch}

Price has moved over the last ten minutes while the nowcast has
not, or has moved the other way.  Local \(z\) is large and
percent-via-price on later marks stays near 0 if the line later
catches up.  The working idea: the stretch is not in the recent
prints.  V1's session banner may still say AGGRESS DOMINANT about
the day; this page is only about the last ten minutes.

\subsection{Impulse rewrite}

A one-minute burst of \code{agg_1m} arrives, the dashed line jumps,
and local \(z\) dies without TY giving back the ticks.  The working
idea: the nowcast just absorbed the new flow.  The old leftover was
not a target.  Wait for the new stretch to form.

\subsection{Quiet local tape}

Live state is NONE, ENTRY is FLAT, residual \(z\) is inside 1.5, and
session ticks are either missing or inside 2.  The last ten minutes
are consistent enough that this page has nothing extra to say.
LIFO FRIENDLY can still appear if \(\abs{z}\ge 1\) with quiet
trade intensity; that is a range annotation, not an entry.

\subsection{Nowcast-only heat}

Local \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) but the strip says \code{SKIP NOWCAST_ONLY}.
The 10-minute model is extended; V2's session residual is under 2
ticks.  The 2026-09-02 tape was mostly this case.  Treat it as
nowcast chatter, not a session dislocation.

\subsection{Gated disagreement}

Local and session signs differ (\code{DISAGREE}), or session ticks
exceed 6 (\code{REGIME_TAIL}), or the last exit still owns this
session sign (\code{SAME_SIGN_OPEN}).  The page is working.  It is
refusing a second look at the same session residual.

\subsection{Open paper clock}

ENTRY names SHORT or LONG at the open last print.  That strip is
the diagnostic scoreboard, not the goal of the page.  NOW counts
minutes held, whether \(z\)-exit is armed, and time to the
10-minute mark.  If the closed row's price share of reversion is
near 0, the leftover died because the nowcast moved --- the usual
outcome, and the reason this is not a fade monitor.  Share near 1
means price moved toward the old line; treat that as a description
of that episode, not a rule.

\subsection{Z-reset versus flip}

Z-RESET: the local lean died on the fade side after five minutes.
FLIP: the other side printed \(\abs{z}\ge 1\) first.  Flip during the
min-hold is allowed because the opposite lean is already live.  Neither
exit is a fill.

\subsection{10-minute mark still open}

The chart can show 10M MARK while NOW says the trade is still holding.
That is intended.  Score the 5m/10m columns; do not invent a flatten.

\subsection{Session banner versus calendar day}

Today's table is the Globex window, not midnight ET.  Overnight after
18:00 is a new session.  The 2026-09-02 12:07 cutover applies only to
that one session's banner and table.

\subsection{LIFO on an open fade}

LIFO FRIENDLY during an open SHORT/LONG is not confluence.  H5 is
non-directional range lean.  The fade clock is directional paper.
They share a \(z\) threshold family and nothing else.

\subsection{Holdout pane versus live pane}

The model/date charts are B1\_W10 holdout predictions from the
2026-08-31 study.  They are not the live Ridge, not the conviction
gate, and not today's tape.  Use them to remember why the construction
exists.
