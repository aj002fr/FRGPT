\begin{abstract}
This note is the companion to the V1 measurement note
(\code{docs/aggression_v1_v22_research_program.tex}).
It assumes that note has already been read: TotalAggress, BuySell
Ratio, the two contemporaneous OLS fair-value lines, implied passive
as \(\Delta e\), and the flow-dominance banner are taken as known.
Nothing here re-derives those constructions.

V1 asked whether a TY price move was in the aggressive curve prints.
The leftover after that fit---the residual---was a description, not an
object to trade.  V2.2 and V2.7 are isolated research pages that take
that leftover seriously.  V2.2 keeps a session-scale multi-feature
Ridge of same-day price change, fades large residuals, and overlays
the non-directional H5 range lean labeled LIFO FRIENDLY.
V2.7 replaces the session line with a 10-minute local nowcast
(\code{B1_W10}) and adds a paper clock that only opens when that
local lean agrees with a modest V2 session residual.

Neither page is a trading system.  Neither emits executable long or
short.  Neither has an after-cost PASS.  Live V1 on port 5050, live V2
on 5052, and every other aggression monitor stay unchanged.
\end{abstract}

\section{How to read this note}

Read the V1 note first.  This document starts at the question V1
could not answer and then specifies two later pages in enough detail
that a first-time reader can reconstruct every number they see.

The lineage is linear:

\begin{center}
V1 measurement
\;\(\longrightarrow\)\;
V2 session Ridge residual
\;\(\longrightarrow\)\;
V2.2 isolated copy + LIFO FRIENDLY
\;\(\longrightarrow\)\;
V2.7 isolated copy + local 10-minute nowcast.
\end{center}

V2 itself is the research ancestor, not a page this note documents
end-to-end.  V2.2 \emph{is} that ancestor, copied onto its own port
and store, plus one overlay.  V2.7 is a copy of V2.2 with the fair
value and the clock swapped.  Named V2.7 because V2.3 already belongs
to the move-exhaustion monitor on port 5059.

Two ideas stay fixed from V1 through both later pages:

\begin{itemize}
    \item A positive residual still means price is rich versus the
    fitted line of that page.  A negative residual still means cheap.
    \item ``Implied passive'' and ``fair value'' remain
    contemporaneous descriptions.  They are not forecasts of the next
    print, and they are not resting-order identity.
\end{itemize}

What changes is the line being subtracted, the features that build
it, and whether the leftover is only drawn or also turned into a
paper episode.


\section{The question V1 left open}

V1's job is measurement.  Panel~4 fits
\(P_t=\alpha+\beta A_t+e_t\) once per session on TotalAggress
\(A_t\).  The banner then splits each completed minute's price change
into \(\beta\Delta A_t\) (aggress) and \(\Delta e_t\) (implied
passive) and names a winner.  That answers: \emph{is this move in the
prints?}

It does not answer three follow-on questions that a researcher
watching the same tape naturally asks:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item If price is rich or cheap versus that line, does the gap
    close, and does it close because price moves or because the line
    chases?
    \item Is a one-feature OLS on session TotalAggress the most
    useful line, or does quote-at-trade context (spread, depth, size
    mix, wing flow) change the leftover?
    \item When the leftover is large \emph{and} the tape is quiet,
    is the next half-hour more often a continued range than a
    breakout?
\end{enumerate}

Question~1 is a residual-as-object question.  V1 never opened a
paper trade, never scored a fade, and never wrote a live store.
Question~2 is a specification question: same residual idea, richer
right-hand side, still contemporaneous.  Question~3 is an environment
question, not a long/short.  It came from the offline V1
flow--dispersion foundations study, not from the live V1 banner.

V2.2 is the live page that takes (1) and (2) together and hangs (3)
on as a banner.  V2.7 is the later page that asks V1's measurement
question on the last ten minutes: did this short stretch of TY
match the short stretch of prints?  The session residual was too
flippy to watch as a 5--10~minute leftover, so the line was
shortened.  The leftover is still a consistency check, not a
target.


\section{The V2 step, in one sitting}

Before V2.2 can be specified, the V2 construction it copies has to be
named.  This section is the bridge, not a second V1-style manual for
live V2 (port 5052).

V1's fair value is a same-session OLS of price on one flow sum.
V2 keeps the leftover idea and changes three things.

\paragraph{Target.}
Raw price is a level.  A regression on a level can ride the session
drift.  V2 therefore fits the same-day price change:

\begin{equation}
  y^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  =
  P_t - P_{\mathrm{open}},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{FV}^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  =
  P_{\mathrm{open}}
  +
  \mathrm{Ridge}(X^{\mathrm{session}}_t,\, y^{\mathrm{session}}),
  \qquad
  e^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  =
  P_t - \mathrm{FV}^{\mathrm{session}}_t.
\end{equation}

\(P_t\) is the TY last print in the completed one-minute bar.
\(P_{\mathrm{open}}\) is the first TY last-print in the current live
feature frame.  Cumulative flow in that frame is also reset to zero
at the first bar.  That is the operational analogue of V1's
session-from-open TotalAggress; it is not a second 18:00~ET clock
inside the feature file.

\paragraph{Features.}
The right-hand side is no longer only \(A_t\).  V2 builds a
quote-at-trade pack from FV, TU, TY, and US: session cumulative
signed DV01 flow, 5- and 30-minute flow, BuySell Ratio, trade
intensity, size mix, wing 5-minute flow, spread and depth at the
print, 30-minute realized vol, 5-minute momentum, and minutes since
01:00~ET.  The exact list is in the V2.2 feature section below.
Aggressor sign, DV01 weights, and the BuySell EMA are the V1
mappings; they are not re-derived here.

\paragraph{Estimator.}
Ridge with a StandardScaler, alphas
\(\{0.01,0.1,1,10,100\}\), fit once per UTC date on the published
TBBO matrix, not on the live buffer.  OLS was competitive on
in-sample fit; Ridge is what the live residual signal uses because
the features are collinear.

\paragraph{What the research found.}
On 54{,}835 clean rows / 74 dates, continuous residuals were not a
forecast.  Sparse extremes were the interesting object: fade
\(\operatorname{sign}(e)\) when \(\abs{z}\ge 2\) and hold 30 minutes
had an 81\% holdout hit on last-print direction.  That is a research
letter, not an after-cost PASS.  The later 2026-07-09 forward-return
and EV gates stayed negative after costs.  V2.2 inherits the display
and the paper scoreboard; it does not inherit a ship decision.

The reading shift from V1 is therefore this: V1 asks whether the
\emph{move} is in the prints.  V2 asks whether the \emph{level} is
rich or cheap versus a session-scale flow-and-book line, and whether
that gap later shrinks.

\section{V2.2: session Ridge fair value}

After at least the cleanliness filter on the historical matrix
(500 rows per ET date, price in \((80,150)\), session-target
standard deviation in \((0.01,1)\)), one Ridge is fit for the UTC
calendar date:

\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\Delta}^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  =
  \mathrm{RidgeCV}(X^{\mathrm{session}}_t,\, y^{\mathrm{session}}),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{FV}^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  =
  P_{\mathrm{open}} + \widehat{\Delta}^{\mathrm{session}}_t,
  \qquad
  e^{\mathrm{session}}_t
  =
  P_t - \mathrm{FV}^{\mathrm{session}}_t.
\end{equation}

Positive \(e\): last print is rich versus the session line.
Negative \(e\): cheap.  The paper lean fades \(e\): short when
rich, long when cheap.

Two contrasts with V1 panel~4 matter.

\begin{itemize}
    \item V1 fits \(\alpha+\beta A_t\) on \emph{this} session's
    aligned minutes and caches \(\alpha,\beta\) until the session
    key changes.  V2.2 fits on the historical matrix and applies
    those coefficients to today.  Today's leftover is
    out-of-sample with respect to the live day.
    \item V1's dashed line is a function of one flow sum.  V2.2's
    dashed line is a function of the whole pack.  A large V2.2
    residual means ``price is away from this multi-feature session
    nowcast,'' not ``price is away from TotalAggress alone.''
\end{itemize}

The live fair-value chart is that dashed line against solid last
print.  The dual-axis visual scaling of V1 panel~1 is not used
here; both series are in price points.


\section{V2.2: residual \(z\)-score}

Alerts use \(z\), not raw \(e\).  Method \code{prior_blend_v1}:

\begin{align}
  \mu_{\mathrm{hist}} &= \text{training-residual mean}, \\
  \sigma_{\mathrm{hist}} &= \text{median of per-day residual std.\ (positive days)}, \\
  w_t &= \frac{60}{60 + n_t},
\end{align}

where \(n_t\) is the expanding count of live residuals so far in
the current frame.  The live mean and std.\ use an expanding window
with a 30-point minimum.  Until that minimum, the historical
moments stand in.

\begin{equation}
  \mu_t = w_t\,\mu_{\mathrm{hist}} + (1-w_t)\,\mu^{\mathrm{today}}_t,
  \qquad
  \sigma_t^2 = w_t\,\sigma_{\mathrm{hist}}^2 + (1-w_t)\,(\sigma^{\mathrm{today}}_t)^2,
  \qquad
  z_t = \frac{e_t - \mu_t}{\sigma_t}.
\end{equation}

This is not V1's \(z^{\mathrm{V1,live}}=e/s_e\) on the current
aligned frame, and it is not the banner's \(z_A,z_P\).  Early in
the day the historical prior dominates, so a launch at 10:00~ET
does not invent a new scale.

Live banner states from \(\abs{z}\) alone:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\(\abs{z}\) & State \\
\midrule
\(< 1.5\) & \code{NONE} \\
\(\ge 1.5\) & \code{WATCH} \\
\(\ge 2.0\) & \code{DISLOCATION} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

WATCH and DISLOCATION are labels on the same clock.  A WATCH can
escalate to DISLOCATION if \(\abs{z}\) later reaches 2.  They are
not two strategies.


\section{V2.2: paper fade clock}

One episode may be open at a time.  TY tick size is \(1/64\).
Direction is fade:

\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{sign}
  =
  \begin{cases}
    -1 & e_t > 0 \quad (\mathrm{SHORT\_RICH}), \\
    +1 & e_t < 0 \quad (\mathrm{LONG\_CHEAP}).
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

A new episode opens when \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) and no episode is
already open.  There is no session-tick gate on V2.2; that filter
arrives only on V2.7.

While open:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Resolved.}
    \(\abs{z}\) falls below 1 before 30 minutes.  The first such
    bar is the close for the signal scoreboard.
    \item \textbf{Expired.}
    Thirty minutes pass with \(\abs{z}\) still at or above 1.
    \item \textbf{15m / 30m marks.}
    Last-print fade P\&L and adverse path are scored at those
    horizons whether or not \(z\) reset.  The 30-minute mark is
    also the deadline that finalizes the episode.
\end{itemize}

Paper P\&L on a path is \(\mathrm{sign}\times(P-P_{\mathrm{open}})\).
Positive means the fade made last-print ticks.

Two scoreboards can disagree, and that disagreement is the point.
Resolved means the \emph{model gap} closed (\(\abs{z}<1\)).
Thirty-minute hit means \emph{price} moved the fade way.  Fair
value can chase price (resolved, little PnL).  Price can pay the
fade while \(z\) stays elevated (hit, still open or expired).

Hold-expectation cards report, for resolved episodes: median
minutes to \(\abs{z}<1\), median adverse ticks before that, and
price share of reversion

\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{share}
  =
  \mathrm{clip}_{[-1,2]}
  \left(
    \mathrm{sign}(e_{\mathrm{open}})
    \frac{P_{\mathrm{open}}-P_{\mathrm{close}}}{\abs{e_{\mathrm{open}}}}
  \right)
\end{equation}

when \(\abs{e_{\mathrm{open}}}\) is at least one tick.  Share near
0 means the nowcast moved to price (FV chase).  Share near 1 means
price moved to the nowcast (the fade the clock claims).

These are last-print research marks.  They are not fills, not
bid/ask, and not after-cost.


\section{V2.2: LIFO FRIENDLY}
\label{sec:lifo}

Display-only.  Not a second entry clock.  Not long or short.

The rule comes from the 2026-07-17 V1 foundations study, cell
\code{H5_trade_int_lo}.  On the primary 30-minute / 8~bps / \(k=1\)
range-versus-breakout label, low trade intensity while the residual
was large predicted CONTINUED\_RANGE: validation restrictive PASS
at \(+10.6\)~pp, FINAL lift \(+8.15\)~pp, one-sided CI failed.
That is why the overlay may be shown and may not be shipped.

V2.2 applies the same inequality to \emph{its own} session
\(z\) and V2 trade intensity, not to V1's panel-4 \(z\):

\begin{equation}
  \abs{z_t}\ge 1
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \text{trade intensity}_t
  \le
  \text{session expanding } p_{33}
  \text{ (prior bars, min.\ 20)}.
\end{equation}

The quantile is causal: it is computed on earlier bars of the same
ET date, then compared to the current bar.  Until 20 prior bars
exist, the low-intensity leg cannot fire.

UI text is only \textbf{LIFO FRIENDLY}.
Label
\code{PROVISIONAL / LIFO-FRIENDLY / RANGE-LEAN / NON-DIRECTIONAL}.
The stats card counts finalized LIFO episodes scored as RANGE /
TREND / ambiguous over a 30-minute forward window, plus how many
are active.  Those counts are observational.  They do not promote
the overlay.

``LIFO'' here is an environment word: a quiet tape with a large
leftover is friendlier to last-in-first-out / mean-reversion style
participation than to a breakout chase.  It is not an inventory
instruction.


\section{Why V2.7 existed}

V1 already answers the session-scale question: is today's move in
the prints?  A researcher watching a single burst still needs the
same question on that burst.  Session Ridge leftover is the wrong
object for that.  It is a session-scale level.  On the 2026-08-31
short-horizon study, the raw sign of that residual flipped about
every two minutes (7.0 flips/hour).  You cannot read a 10-minute
stretch against a leftover that dies in two.

The useful replacement is not a better fade.  It is a nowcast of
the stretch just taken: did the last \(W\) minutes of TY match the
last \(W\) minutes of flow?  Ten minutes is the window that still
fits that stretch without the \(W=5\) leak, and that lasts long
enough to look at.  It is not a claim that ten minutes is true
fair value.

Three tracks were run on the published TBBO matrix, walk-forward
by ET date, locked holdout 2026-05-13 through 2026-06-05
(15 days).  Live V2.2 was not retuned.

\paragraph{Track A.}
A forward Ridge of \(P_{t+h}-P_t\).  Sign skill existed
(1-minute AUC 0.67).  \(R^2\) lift versus random walk was of order
\(10^{-5}\).  That does not draw a useful new price line.

\paragraph{Track B.}
A rolling-anchor nowcast of the last \(W\) minutes.  Pack B1
(short flow, no \code{agg_30m}) beat the session pack except at
\(W=5\), which is invalid: live \code{mom_5m} \emph{is} the local
target at that window (\(R^2=1\), specification leak).  \(W=10\)
does not have that leak.  Holdout local-target \(R^2\) for
B1\_W10 was 0.724; tracking MAE 0.85 ticks.  Fade-the-residual
hit rates stayed near 51--52\%.  The nowcast explains the last
ten minutes.  It does not, by itself, pick the next ten.

\paragraph{Track C.}
Hysteresis on those residuals, aimed at a frozen hold profile
(typical hold 5--10 minutes, typical wrong-way about 3 ticks,
too flippy if lean duration \(<3\) minutes, too sticky if
\(>20\)).  The sweet-spot cell was B1\_W10 with enter
\(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\), fade-side exit through 0.5 after a five-minute
min-hold.  Median lean 5.5 minutes; 5.81 flips/hour versus session
raw 6.98; share of 10-minute windows with \(\ge 3\) changes 4.3\%

\section{V2.7: B1\_W10}

Let \(P_t\) be the TY last print in the completed 1-minute bar.
The local anchor is the print ten minutes earlier:

\begin{equation}
  A_t = P_{t-10},
  \qquad
  y^{\mathrm{B1}}_t = P_t - A_t.
\end{equation}

The first ten bars of a session have no anchor and produce no
prediction.  There is no lookahead: \(A_t\) is a shift of \(P\).

\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\Delta}_t = \mathrm{RidgeCV}(X^{\mathrm{B1}}_t,\, y^{\mathrm{B1}}),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{FV}_t = A_t + \widehat{\Delta}_t,
  \qquad
  e_t = P_t - \mathrm{FV}_t.
\end{equation}

Positive \(e_t\): last print is rich versus the 10-minute nowcast.
Negative \(e_t\): cheap.  The paper lean still fades \(e_t\).

RidgeCV uses the same live V2 alphas and cleanliness filter as
V2.2.  Fit is once per UTC date on the historical matrix after the
local columns are added.  The live buffer is not the training set.

B1 is the short pack.  It is not the session V2 feature list.
\code{agg_30m} and \code{minutes_since_open} are excluded.
\code{minutes_since_anchor} is the constant 10 once the anchor
exists.  \code{agg_cum_10m} is the 10-minute sum of
\code{agg_1m}.  \code{realized_vol_5} is the 5-minute std.\ of
1-minute price changes.  \code{mom_5m} is safe at \(W=10\).

\section{V2.7: conviction entry and exit}

Local \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) is necessary and not sufficient.  Let
\(e^{\mathrm{sess}}_t\) be live V2's session residual on the same
minute, and \(\tau_t = e^{\mathrm{sess}}_t / (1/64)\) its tick
value.  A new episode opens only when all of the following hold
and no episode is already open:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Session residual is present (else \code{SESSION_UNAVAILABLE}).
    \item \(2 \le \abs{\tau_t} \le 6\)
    (else \code{NOWCAST_ONLY} if too small, \code{REGIME_TAIL} if too large).
    \item \(\mathrm{sign}(e_t)=\mathrm{sign}(e^{\mathrm{sess}}_t)\)
    and neither is zero (else \code{DISAGREE}).
    \item That session sign is not the same as the last finalized
    episode's exit session-sign in the current 18:00~ET Globex
    session (else \code{SAME_SIGN_OPEN}).
\end{enumerate}

The live strip then shows
\code{FLAT --- SKIP <reason>}
when \(z\) is hot but gated, or the wait text
``FLAT --- wait for \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\), session 2--6 ticks, same sign,
new session-sign after exit.''

This gate is an alert-rate filter from the 2026-09-02 correction
tape.  It is not an after-cost PASS.  C1 (\(\abs{\tau}\ge 2\)) was
persistence on that tape, not expectancy.

Once open:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{EXIT Z-RESET.}
    After five minutes, the fade side dies:
    short exits at \(z\le 0.5\), long at \(z\ge -0.5\).
    \item \textbf{EXIT FLIP.}
    Opposite \(\abs{z}\ge 1\), even during the five-minute min-hold.
    Short flips at \(z\le -1\); long flips at \(z\ge +1\).
    \item \textbf{10M MARK.}
    Ten minutes after entry is scored (5-minute and 10-minute path
    marks) and drawn on the chart.  The trade stays open.  A
    \(z\)-reset or flip still closes it after the mark.
\end{itemize}

A closed trade frees the next entry, subject to the same-sign
lockout for the rest of the 18:00 session.  The lockout is on
session-residual sign, not on local \(z\).

Each entry\(\rightarrow\)exit is one row.  Table \textbf{trade pnl}
is last-print fade from the open last print to the exit last
print.  \textbf{Trade adverse} is the worst fade-path mark between
those two prints.

The session-day banner sums \code{dir_pnl_trade_ticks} over
finalized rows in the visible window.  Open trades count as
issued/open and do not enter ticks until they close.  Win / lose /
flat split those closed tick values at zero.

Globex session open is the most recent 18:00~ET at or before now.
Same-sign re-entry always uses that full session.  The banner and
Today's table use a one-session cutover: for the Globex day that
contains 2026-09-02 12:07~ET, visible stats start there
(conviction-gate restart).  After the next 18:00~ET open they use
the full trading day again.

LIFO FRIENDLY on V2.7 is the same H5 inequality as
Section~\ref{sec:lifo}, evaluated on V2.7's local \(z\) and the
same trade-intensity series.  It is still not confluence with an
open SHORT/LONG.

\section{How to read the two pages}

These are reading habits, not validated states.  Neither banner
becomes a long or short.

\subsection{V2.2: quiet session leftover}

Live state is NONE, \(\abs{z}<1.5\), dashed session line hugs
price.  V1's question (is the move in the prints?) can still be
answered on port 5050.  V2.2 has nothing sparse to fade.

\subsection{V2.2: WATCH / DISLOCATION}

Price is away from the session Ridge.  Ask the two scoreboards
separately.  If Hold Expectations show price share near 0, the
gap is closing because the line is moving.  That is V1's
``unexplained extension'' habit in reverse: the model is catching
the print, not the print reverting.

\subsection{V2.2: LIFO without a fade}

LIFO FRIENDLY can print at \(\abs{z}\ge 1\) while the fade clock
is still NONE (\(\abs{z}<1.5\)).  That is intended.  H5 is a range
environment at a lower threshold.  It is not permission to fade.

\subsection{V2.7: flow-explained stretch}

Last print and the 10-minute nowcast travel together.  The burst
is in the recent prints.  The useful read is to stop inventing a
fade.  V1 may still be naming a session-scale winner; this page
is only about the last ten minutes.

\subsection{V2.7: unexplained local stretch}

Price ran over the last ten minutes while the nowcast did not.
Something other than the recent curve prints is carrying TY.  If
the dashed line later catches the print, that is an impulse
rewrite, not a successful fade.

\subsection{V2.7: nowcast-only heat}

Local \(\abs{z}\ge 1.5\) but the strip says \code{SKIP NOWCAST_ONLY}.
The 10-minute model is extended; V2's session residual is under 2
ticks.  The 2026-09-02 tape was mostly this case.  Treat it as
nowcast chatter, not a session dislocation.

\subsection{V2.7: gated disagreement}

Local and session signs differ (\code{DISAGREE}), or session ticks
exceed 6 (\code{REGIME_TAIL}), or the last exit still owns this
session sign (\code{SAME_SIGN_OPEN}).  The page is working.  It is
refusing a second look at the same session residual.

\subsection{V2.7: open paper clock}

ENTRY names SHORT or LONG at the open last print.  That strip is
the diagnostic scoreboard, not the goal.  If the closed row's
price share is near 0, the leftover died because the nowcast
moved --- the usual outcome.  Do not read ENTRY as a trade.

\subsection{V2.7: 10-minute mark still open}

The chart can show 10M MARK while NOW says the trade is still
holding.  That is intended.  Score the 5m/10m columns; do not
invent a flatten.

\subsection{V2.7: LIFO on an open fade}

LIFO FRIENDLY during an open SHORT/LONG is not confluence.  H5 is
non-directional range lean.  The fade clock is directional paper.
They share a \(z\) threshold family and nothing else.

\subsection{Both pages versus V1}

If V1 is AGGRESS DOMINANT and V2.2/V2.7 \(z\) is small, the move
is in the prints and the leftover pages have little to say.  If
V1 is IMPLIED PASSIVE DOMINANT and V2.7 is SKIP NOWCAST\_ONLY, the
session leftover is still small in ticks: V1 is naming a
contribution winner, not a 2-tick dislocation.  If V1 is QUIET and
V2.7 opens a fade, trust the gate: session residual is 2--6 ticks
even if V1's 15-row \(z_A,z_P\) are both under 0.5.  The two
\(z\) families are different objects.

\subsection{Holdout pane versus live pane}

On both pages the model/date charts are historical walk-forward
drawings.  They are not today's Ridge, not the conviction gate,
and not today's tape.  Use them to remember why the construction
exists.
